# AdventureWorks Sales Analytics Platform

**SQL · Power BI · Python · Databricks · PySpark · AWS S3 · Airflow**

## Project Overview

**Adventure Works Cycles** is the fictional bicycle manufacturer and retailer represented in Microsoft's AdventureWorks dataset. The company sells bicycles and related products across multiple customers, product categories, sales territories, and sales representatives.

### The Problem

Adventure Works had detailed operational sales data, but the information was distributed across multiple tables covering **orders, customers, products, salespeople, and territories**.

This made it difficult for management to quickly answer important business questions:

- How is revenue changing over time?
- Which products and categories drive sales?
- Who are the company's most valuable customers?
- Which customers are becoming inactive?
- Which territories are growing or declining?
- Which salespeople are outperforming their regional benchmarks?

> **Business challenge:** Transform fragmented operational data into a centralized analytics solution that management can use to understand commercial performance and make data-driven decisions.

---

# Solution

I built an **end-to-end sales analytics platform** that transforms raw AdventureWorks data into business-ready insights.

The solution follows this workflow:

```text
AdventureWorks Data
        ↓
      AWS S3
        ↓
 Python Ingestion
        ↓
    Databricks
 Bronze → Silver → Gold
        ↓
   Star Schema
        ↓
  SQL Analytics
        ↓
     Power BI
        ↓
 Business Insights
```

**Apache Airflow** orchestrates the pipeline.

The solution combines:

- **Python** for ingestion and validation
- **AWS S3** for raw data storage
- **Databricks + PySpark** for data transformation
- **Delta Lake** for analytical storage
- **Star Schema** dimensional modeling
- **SQL** for business analysis
- **Power BI** for dashboards and decision support
- **Apache Airflow** for workflow orchestration

# Analysis & Results

The transformed data was used to analyze Adventure Works' commercial performance across **sales, customers, products, territories, and salespeople**.

## Sales Performance

![Executive Sales Dashboard](docs/dashboards/executive_sales_dashboard.png)

The analysis provides visibility into:

- Monthly revenue trends
- Month-over-month growth
- Seasonal sales patterns
- Sales performance by day of the week
- Periods of increasing or declining sales

**Result:** Management can quickly identify when sales performance improves or deteriorates and investigate the business areas driving those changes.

---

## Customer Performance

![Customer Analytics](docs/dashboards/customer_analytics.png)

Customer-level analysis identifies:

- Highest-value customers by lifetime revenue
- Customer revenue concentration
- Customers at risk of churn
- Customers inactive for 12+ months
- Average purchase intervals

**Result:** Adventure Works can distinguish its most valuable customers from customers showing declining engagement, supporting better retention and customer-prioritization decisions.

---

## Customer Segmentation

![Customer Segmentation](docs/dashboards/customer_segmentation.png)

I implemented **RFM Analysis (Recency, Frequency, Monetary Value)** to segment customers according to purchasing behavior.

The analysis compares:

- Customer distribution by segment
- Revenue generated by segment
- Customer value across segments

**Result:** Raw transaction history is converted into actionable customer groups that can support targeted marketing, retention, loyalty, and re-engagement strategies.

---

## Product Performance

![Product Analytics](docs/dashboards/product_analytics.png)

Product analysis evaluates:

- Revenue by product category
- Category contribution to total revenue
- Top-performing products
- Frequently purchased product combinations
- High-revenue products with relatively low margins

**Result:** Adventure Works can identify the products and categories driving revenue while uncovering opportunities for cross-selling, product bundling, and margin optimization.

---

## Territory Performance

![Sales Territory](docs/dashboards/sales_territory.png)

Geographic analysis compares:

- Revenue by territory
- Year-over-year growth
- Current-year vs. previous-year sales

**Result:** Management can identify high-performing markets, territories losing momentum, and regions with potential growth opportunities.

---

## Salesperson Performance

![Salesperson Performance](docs/dashboards/salesperson_performance.png)

Sales representatives are evaluated using:

- Revenue by salesperson
- Territory average revenue
- Salesperson vs. territory benchmark

**Result:** Performance can be evaluated relative to each salesperson's market rather than relying only on total sales, making it easier to identify above- and below-benchmark performers.

The project transformed fragmented AdventureWorks operational data into a **centralized sales analytics platform**.

Instead of analyzing individual operational tables, business users can now evaluate the company through six decision-focused Power BI dashboards covering:

**Sales → Customers → Segmentation → Products → Territories → Salespeople**

The final solution demonstrates how raw bicycle sales data can be transformed into **structured, actionable business intelligence** while maintaining an automated end-to-end data pipeline.
